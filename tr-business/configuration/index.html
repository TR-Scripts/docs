Configuration

Previous Next

Every setting in config.lua and config.server-only.lua

config.lua holds the settings below. Business types, the Discord webhook and bot token, and Config.Functions go in config.server-only.lua.

General¶

Setting Default What it does
Config.Debug false Prints every logged action to the server console.
Config.Webhook false A Discord webhook for logs: cash, sales, staff, opening and closing.
Config.RegisterDistance 1.5 How close to the register for the Manage prompt.
Config.CloseIdleOnStart 60 Seconds after server start to close every open business that nobody can serve (no clerk hired, clerk sent home, or no clerk spot), or whose clerk has nothing priced in stock (even if it's buying, unless Config.StayOpenWhileBuying). A buy-only business stays open while it has something to buy that the register can pay for. Stops abandoned businesses staying open forever. Businesses whose storage isn't ready yet are left open. false = off.
Config.AllowMovement false Let players move while the management menu is open.
Config.Prompts.Manage "INPUT_INTERACT_OPTION1" The Manage key.
Config.Prompts.Buy "INPUT_INTERACT_OPTION1" The Shop key at the clerk.
Config.BlipModifiers { open = false, closed = "BLIP_MODIFIER_OUTSIDE_TOD" } The blip modifier for an owned business when open or closed, in place of tr-properties' owned. false for none.
Config.CounterCam The camera on the player while they manage, and on the clerk while a customer shops: offset, lookAt, fov, easeMs. shoulder (offset, lookAt, fov) frames the over-the-shoulder register camera for properties with counterCam = "shoulder"; its lookAt is a point ahead of the player.
Config.YearModifier 0 Years added to dates in the shift log, e.g. -122 for 1900s RP.
Config.Names { min = 3, max = 40 } How long a business's name can be.
Config.Keys { cut = 15.0, change = 50.0 } Fees for cutting a key and changing the locks, from the player's cash. false turns one off.

Ranks and permissions¶

Config.Ranks = { "staff", "manager" }   -- lowest first
Config.RankLabels = { staff = "Staff", manager = "Manager", owner = "Owner" }

The owner (the owning character, or members of the owning job) is above every rank. Each permission names the lowest rank allowed, or false to turn it off:

Permission Default What it allows
toggleOpen "staff" Open and close the business, and send the clerk home or call them back.
deposit "staff" Put money in the register.
withdraw "manager" Take money out.
staff "owner" Hire, fire, promote and demote. false on a type with roles: ranks come from job grades only, and anyone hired before no longer counts. Hours and shift logs still show under shifts.
rename "owner" Rename the business.
describe "owner" Change the description.
image "owner" Change the image.
keys "owner" Cut keys and change the locks.
clockIn "staff" Clock in at the register. Anyone can clock out.
shifts "manager" See hours and shift logs in the staff list.
prices "manager" Set what the storefront sells and for how much. false makes the storefront buy-only.
buying "manager" Set what the clerk buys from players, for how much, and up to how many.
clerk "owner" Hire and dismiss the NPC clerk.
ledger "owner" Read the ledger at the register.

Config.JobPermissions overrides these for businesses owned by a job; by default it turns off rename and keys. A business type's permissions override both.

Staff and shifts¶

Setting Default What it does
Config.Staff.max 10 Hired staff per business.
Config.Staff.hireDistance 5.0 How close the new hire must stand.
Config.Unattended.closeAfter 5 With no clerk serving, minutes after the last person on shift leaves (clocks out, logs off, loses the job) before the business closes. Clocking in or a clerk serving cancels it. The owner is told if they're online. false = stay open. A business type can set its own closeUnattended.
Config.Unattended.clockInOnOpen true Opening, or sending the clerk home, with nobody serving clocks you in, unless you're on shift at another business or can't clock in here.
Config.StayOpenWhileBuying false true: at server start, a shop with nothing in stock stays open while its clerk is buying something the register can pay for. A business type can set its own stayOpenWhileBuying.
Config.LogBatch 30 Seconds to wait after a player's last purchase or sale at the counter before logging it. Buying or selling the same item again within that time adds to one entry ("Apple x12", with the total) in the log, the webhook and the ledger. false = one entry per click.
Config.Ledger.perPage 25 Ledger entries per page. Each row has a Page slider, as in tr-mail's directory.
Config.Shifts.block 15 Minutes credited at a time: one block on clocking in, then one per block worked.
Config.Shifts.check 30 Seconds between checks for due blocks.
Config.Shifts.perPage 10 Shifts per page in a member's log. Each row has a Page slider, as in tr-mail's directory.
Config.Shifts.timeFormat "%H:%M" os.date format for clock-in and clock-out times.

Storefront¶

Config.Shop = {
    sellItems = false,      -- what the storefront sells: a list of item names, or false for anything
    buyItems = false,       -- what the clerk buys from players: a list, or false for anything
    distance = 2.0,         -- how close to the clerk for the Buy prompt
    spawnDistance = 40.0,   -- the clerk appears for players this close
    maxPrice = 1000.0,      -- highest price per item
    allowSellWeapons = false, -- sell weapons from storage too
    sellCustomWeapons = false, -- also sell customized weapons
    buyMaxStock = 100,      -- highest "buy up to" staff can set per item
}

Config.Clerk = {
    hire = 200.0,           -- paid from the register to hire one
    wage = 25.0,            -- from the register each server start; can't pay = they quit. false = none
    cut = 0.3,              -- share of every sale the clerk keeps
    scenario = "WORLD_HUMAN_SHOPKEEPER", -- what they do at the counter
    options = {             -- who can be hired
        { name = "Steve", model = "U_M_M_ValGenStoreOwner_01" },
        { name = "Caroline", model = "U_F_M_TumGeneralStoreOwner_01" },
        { name = "Eustace", model = "U_M_M_StrGenStoreOwner_01" },
    },
}

See Storefront.

Business types¶

Settings shared by every business of a type. A property opts in with businessType = "sheriff".

Config.BusinessTypes = {
    sheriff = {
        label = "Sheriff's Department",
        singleThread = true,
        description = "Law and order across the territory.",
        image = "https://i.imgur.com/....png",
        roles = {
            owner = { job = "leo", minGrade = 4 },
            manager = { job = "leo", minGrade = 3 },
            staff = { job = "leo", minGrade = 1 },
        },
        permissions = { deposit = "owner", withdraw = "owner", staff = false, prices = false, clerk = false },
        onShift = function(src, id, block) -- e.g. pay by grade, only while they still hold the job
            if jo.framework:getJob(src) == "leo" then ... end
        end,
    },
}
Field What it does
label, description, image The shared Discord post, with singleThread.
singleThread true: every business of this type shares one Discord post. See Discord.
discord false: no Discord post for this type's businesses. A property's own discord overrides it.
roles [rank] = { job, minGrade }: job members get the most senior rank their grade reaches instead of all being owners. An owning character is still the owner; hired staff keep a higher hired rank, unless staff is false.
permissions Overrides Config.Permissions and Config.JobPermissions, only the keys listed. Set prices and clerk to false for offices with no storefront, or only prices for one that only buys.
sellItems, buyItems This type's whitelists for selling and buying, overriding Config.Shop's. See What can be sold.
allowSellWeapons true or false: whether this type's storefront sells weapons, overriding Config.Shop.allowSellWeapons.
sellCustomWeapons true or false: whether this type's storefront also sells customized weapons, overriding Config.Shop.sellCustomWeapons. See Weapons.
buyMaxStock The highest "buy up to" staff can set per item for this type, overriding Config.Shop.buyMaxStock.
clerks Who can be hired as this type's clerk, replacing Config.Clerk.options (same shape). A property's own clerks overrides it. See Clerks.
clerkScenario What this type's clerks do at the counter, overriding Config.Clerk.scenario. A property's own clerkScenario overrides it.
stayOpenWhileBuying true or false: this type's Config.StayOpenWhileBuying.
closeUnattended Minutes, or false: this type's Config.Unattended.closeAfter, e.g. false for offices that stay open during set hours.
onClockIn(src, id, rank) Runs on clocking in.
onShift(src, id, block) Runs for each block credited, the first right on clocking in, e.g. to pay wages.
onClockOut(src, id, reason) Runs on clocking out. reason is "clocked out", "logged off" (also on switching character), "moved", "no longer staff" or "changed hands".

Functions¶

The parts that depend on your framework or bank, server side:

Function What it does
CharName(charid) A character's name, also when they're offline. The default reads VORP's characters table.
PayOut(charid, amount) Pays a sold business's float to its old owner, offline too. Return true if paid.
ClockIn(src, id, businessType, rank) Runs for every clock-in, before the type's onClockIn. Use it to put players on duty in your job scripts.
ClockOut(src, id, businessType, reason) Runs for every clock-out. src may already be gone when reason is "logged off".

Text¶

Every text players see (menus, prompts, notifications, the ledger's wording and the Discord post's fallback text) is in config/lang.lua: Lang for menus and prompts, Notifs for notifications, and Lang.LedgerEntries for the ledger. Don't edit lang.lua; set what you want to change in one of these, which load after it:

File Use it for
config/overwriteLang.lua Your changes, kept through updates.
config/lang.server-only.lua Your own server's text. Like config.server-only.lua, it's kept out of copies of the script you share. 
Lang.Shop = "Store"
Notifs.ClockedIn = "Welcome to the shift."
Lang.LedgerEntries.sale = "Sold %s"

%s, %d and %.2f are filled in by the script (a name, a number, an amount): keep them, in the same order.
